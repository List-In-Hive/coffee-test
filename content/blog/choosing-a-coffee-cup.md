## Think about your usual drink
A small espresso and a long filter coffee need different amounts of space. Pick a cup that fits what you usually make.

## Notice how it feels
A comfortable handle, a pleasant rim and a stable base are useful everyday details. Choose something that feels good in your hand.

## Make it your own
Your favourite cup can be simple. The point is to enjoy using it, not to follow a set of rules.
