You can keep a coffee grinder clean without deep disassembly. Most of the work is light brushing and wiping, and you only remove parts your manufacturer says you can take off yourself, such as the hopper. These steps are written mainly for burr grinders; blade grinders differ, so follow that manual instead. Whatever you own, your grinder’s manual has the final word.

## After each use: empty and wipe

Tip out any grounds left in the catch cup or bin, and wipe away coffee dust around the outlet with a dry cloth. That is enough: the aim is to leave less tidying for tomorrow, not to keep the grinder spotless.

## Know when the grinder needs more attention

Over time, coffee oils and fine particles can collect inside a grinder, particularly around the burrs and exit chute. For a broader look at the subject from a grinder maker, see [Baratza’s guide to grinder cleaning essentials](https://www.baratza.com/en-us/blog/cleaning/grinder-cleaning-essentials).

A few signs suggest it may be time for a closer look:

- visible coffee dust or oily residue
- old grounds lingering in the chute
- grinding that seems slower than usual
- grounds that look different at your usual setting
- a cup that tastes unlike your familiar baseline

That last sign is easier to spot if you keep your recipe steady, as in [how to brew more consistent coffee at home](/blog/make-home-coffee-more-consistent). Treat these signs as prompts to check and clean, not as proof that something is wrong.

## How to clean a coffee grinder with a dry brush

For many burr grinders, a gentle dry clean covers most needs:

1. **Read the cleaning section of your manual.** It shows which parts are designed to come off. The [instruction manual for Breville’s conical burr grinder](https://www.breville.com/content/dam/breville/ca/en/assets/miscellaneous/instruction-manual/coffee-grinders/BCG400-instruction-manual.pdf) is one example of where this guidance lives.
2. **Empty the beans** from the hopper as your manual describes.
3. **Unplug the grinder** before reaching inside.
4. **Remove only what your manual allows,** such as the hopper or, on some models, the upper burr.
5. **Brush gently with a soft, dry brush.** Clear the burrs, burr chamber and exit chute only where the brush reaches without moving fixed parts or pushing beyond the access your manual describes.

Stop there unless your manufacturer directs otherwise. Leave deeper disassembly, lower burrs and recalibration to the maker’s instructions. If a part resists, do not force it.

## Can you wash coffee grinder parts?

Sometimes, but only the parts your manual explicitly says are washable. Care rules vary by model, so a part that can be rinsed on one grinder may need to stay dry on another. Fellow’s [cleaning and maintenance guide for the Ode Brew Grinder](https://help.fellowproducts.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402414279309-How-to-clean-maintain-your-Ode-Brew-Grinder) is one example of model-specific care guidance.

Otherwise, keep water away from burrs, the motor and internal parts, and let any washed parts dry completely before putting them back.

## How often to clean your grinder

There is no single schedule for every grinder, but each level of cleaning has a clear boundary:

- **After each use:** the empty-and-wipe reset. No unplugging or disassembly.
- **When you notice buildup:** unplug and do the approved dry-brush clean.
- **At the interval your manual sets:** any deeper cleaning, exactly as the maker describes.

How often you reach the later levels depends on how much you grind. For the rest of your kit, such as drippers, cafetières and cups, [a low-effort coffee equipment cleaning routine](/blog/easy-coffee-equipment-cleaning-routine) offers a broader daily and weekly rhythm.

## Put it back together and brew

After cleaning, check that every removable part sits properly, then brew your usual coffee. If grinding still seems slow or the grounds look unusual, return to the manual rather than taking more apart.

Over time, a short brush-out can simply become part of [a simple coffee routine for slower mornings](/blog/a-simple-coffee-routine).
