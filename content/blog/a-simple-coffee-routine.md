## Start with one small change
Choose one brewing method and keep your coffee and water measurements consistent. Changing a single variable makes it easier to notice what you enjoy.

## Give your cup a little attention
Taste the coffee before adding anything. Let it cool slightly and notice how the flavour changes. There is no need to rush or describe every detail.

## Keep the routine easy
Rinse and clean your equipment after use. Put everything back where you can find it tomorrow. A reliable routine should make the morning simpler, not more complicated.
